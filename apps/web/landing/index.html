How KGSM works

From a simple idea to a playable game in four steps.

  1. 1

    Choose a theme

    Pick a genre and describe your world in plain language.

  2. 2

    Generate story and assets

    AI drafts the story opening and pixel-art assets, with each prompt saved.

  3. 3

    Assemble and playtest

    KGSM builds the game from a tested template and lets you play it in the browser.

  4. 4

    Export

    Download a Phaser project as a ZIP, or a Godot 4 project, and keep full ownership.
