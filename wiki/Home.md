# KGSM Wiki

**Kawalight Game Studio Maker (KGSM)** is a studio for making 2D games from
tested templates. Pick a theme and genre, generate or import assets, play the
game in the studio, then export it.

**Live app:** https://basyid.dev/kgsm/ (landing page) and
https://basyid.dev/kgsm/app/ (studio, sign-in required).

## Pages

- [Getting Started](Getting-Started.md): create your first game step by step.
- [Genres and Templates](Genres-and-Templates.md): what each genre plays like.
- [AI Providers](AI-Providers.md): Muse (Meta), Llama presets, mock, and costs.
- [Playtest and Export](Playtest-and-Export.md): test a game, then export it.
- [Architecture](Architecture.md): how the worker, studio, and templates fit.
- [Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting.md): common errors and what to do.
